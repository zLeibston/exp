# 04 跨任务共性归纳

Combine the per-task diagnoses into a concise mechanism-level summary. For each
proposed shared defect, list affected tasks, decisive trace locations, implicated
code, counterexamples and uncertainty. Similar final failures can have different
causes; group by the supported mechanism rather than the outcome label.

Prioritize improvements with strong evidence, useful cross-task coverage and low
risk to successful behavior. Several repetitions of one task do not establish
cross-task recurrence. A well-supported single-task observation can still reveal
a general mechanism; state the narrower evidence rather than inventing commonality.

Check the proposed mechanism against the successful runs and other supplied tasks.
Ask whether its activation condition is observable and whether valid alternative
strategies remain possible. Separate contradictory requirements or unsupported
hypotheses. Choose a focused improvement opportunity rather than combining every
failure into a broad rewrite. Note coverage gaps caused by the available budget.
